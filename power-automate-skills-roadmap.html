Expressions Cheat Sheet

The classic workflow expression language (what you type in the dynamic content editor's "Expression" tab) — plus a look at Power Fx, the Excel-like formula language Microsoft is spreading across the whole Power Platform.

Workflow Definition Language (classic expressions)
Power Fx — the newer, cross-platform formula language

Power Fx is the same formula language behind canvas apps, and it's steadily showing up inside Power Automate too — in the modern designer's Power Fx-based expressions and in Copilot-assisted flow authoring. It reads like Excel instead of function(args), and it's worth learning alongside WDL, not instead of it.

05b

Suggested Weekly Rhythm

Five focused sessions, two rest days. Work one board section per weekday — 20–40 minutes is enough for most cells. Use Saturday for the week's Mini Lab and Capstone, and take Sunday off. Consistency beats marathon sessions: a flow you build today should still make sense to you next week.

06

Practice — Mini Labs

One short, self-contained lab per stage. Each takes 20–40 minutes and uses only the skills from that stage's board cells.

07
